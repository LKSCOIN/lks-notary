LKS Log

Log stream

The public seals of a log stream. Each one is a fingerprint of a batch of log lines, recorded on the LKSCOIN blockchain. The lines themselves never leave their owner's systems, and are not on this page.

Checking this yourself

This page reports what the service holds. To check your logs against the seals without trusting it, use lks_log.py: one file, standard library only.

Seals prove that sealed lines have not changed since. They do not prove that those lines were true when written.
